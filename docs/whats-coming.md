# What's coming

**Last updated: August 2026**

A look at the ideas and themes the team is working on for a future release. This is not a commitment or a release date. Priorities can shift. This page is rewritten as plans evolve.

- **Personal documents** - Users will be able to upload their own documents into a study collection from chat. A personal document is private to the person who uploads it. It stays separate from the collection's shared documents and does not count against the collection's shared document limit.
- **Wider automatic PHI redaction** - Navigator already redacts PHI in source-document page images before they reach the AI vision service. Future work extends automatic redaction to more surfaces across the product.
- **ICH GCP alignment** - Continued work to align Navigator with ICH E6(R3) Good Clinical Practice guidance for technology vendors, building on the compliance gaps closed in this release.
