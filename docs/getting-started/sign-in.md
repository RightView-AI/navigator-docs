# Sign in

Navigator supports two sign-in methods: **magic link** (passwordless email) and **password**.

![Navigator sign-in page](/img/docs/login-page.png)

## Sign in with a magic link

1. Open [sites.rightview.ai/login](https://sites.rightview.ai/login).
2. Make sure **Sign in with Magic Link** is selected.
3. Enter your work email address.
4. Click **Send sign-in link**.
5. Check your inbox for an email from Rightview Research and click the link. The link expires after a short time; request a new one if it has expired.

:::tip First-time users
If your account was just created, you may receive a welcome email with the same magic-link flow.
:::

## Sign in with a password

1. On the login page, click **Sign in with Password**.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Sign in**.

If you forgot your password, click **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox.

## Session timeout

For security, Navigator signs you out after **30 minutes of inactivity**. If this happens, you will see a message on the login page. Sign in again to continue where you left off.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Magic link email not arriving | Check spam/junk folders. Confirm your email matches the one your admin registered. |
| Link expired | Return to the login page and request a new link. |
| Invalid email or password | Reset your password or contact **research@rightview.ai**. |
