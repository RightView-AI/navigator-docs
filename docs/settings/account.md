# Account settings

Manage your password and sign out from the **Settings** page.

## Open settings

Click **Settings** in the sidebar, or go to [sites.rightview.ai/settings](https://sites.rightview.ai/settings).

## Change your password

Password changes apply only if you sign in with email and password (not magic link only).

1. Enter your **current password**.
2. Enter a **new password** (minimum 8 characters).
3. Confirm the new password.
4. Click **Update password**.

## Sign out

Click **Sign out** at the bottom of the settings page, or use the sign-out option in the sidebar. You will return to the login page.

## Your profile

The settings page shows your registered email and role. Contact your administrator if your role or site assignment needs to change.
