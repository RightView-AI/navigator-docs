# Passive capture (experimental)

Passive capture saves pages you open while you work, so Navigator can find billable work your site may have missed. It is an experimental feature of the [Navigator Chrome extension](/extension/chrome-extension). It is on by default for each user, and you can turn it off at any time. It needs the **Finance** module.

## Which pages are saved

- The extension saves the pages you open on the web addresses that a study allows. When a study lists no addresses, it saves every page.
- It never saves pages on your CTMS web address.
- A saved page has no password values and no scripts. The extension never saves cookies.
- The extension saves up to 200 pages for each device each day.

## What is sent

Each saved page is sent to Rightview as the page HTML and a screenshot. Nothing is saved or sent while capture is off.

## How often it is read

Rightview reads the saved pages every 3 hours to find billable work your site may have missed.

## Where results show

Finance reviews the results on the [Finance desk](/finance/finance-desk). The extension does not show a list of saved pages.

## Turn it off

- **For yourself:** open the **Passive capture** view in the side panel and turn off **Save pages while I work**.
- **For a study:** a site admin can turn capture off for each study in **Collection Settings**. The same place sets the CTMS web address.
