# Users & site capacity

Site administrators can invite new teammates and see how their site's usage compares to its plan limits, without waiting on a platform administrator.

## Invite a teammate

1. Open **Users** in the sidebar, on the **Site users** tab.
2. Enter the new teammate's **email** (required) and **name** (optional).
3. If your site has restricted collections, you can check the ones this person needs. Unrestricted collections are already visible to everyone at your site.
4. Click **Add user**.

The new teammate receives a magic-link email to set their password and sign in. Each row in the list shows the person's role and when they last signed in, or **Never logged in**. They're added with the standard **User** role and no modules; contact **support@rightview.ai** to assign a different role. To give them modules, see [Give a user modules](#give-a-user-modules).

## Give a user modules

A user sees **Visits**, **Finance**, and their tabs only when the site has the module and the user has it too.

1. Open **Users** in the sidebar, on the **Site users** tab.
2. Find the user. Use the switches under their name: **Source QC**, **Patients**, **Schedule of Assessments**, **Finance**, and **Pre-screening**. Only modules that your site has show.
3. Turn a switch on or off. The change saves right away and applies on the user's next request.

Administrators and site administrators always get every module of the site, so their rows say **All site modules (role)**. You cannot give a module that your site does not have. A Rightview platform administrator turns modules on for a site. To add a module to your site, contact your Rightview representative. Only a platform administrator changes modules for sponsor administrators and platform administrators.

## Site capacity

Capacity meters show usage against your site's plan limits:

| Meter | Where it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Users** | **Users** page, **Site users** tab |
| **Collections** | **Create New Study Collection** dialog |
| **Documents** | **Add document** in **Study Documents**, for the selected study |
| **Source documents** | **Visits → Source Quality Control** |

- If your site is at its user limit, **Add user** is disabled. Navigator shows who to contact for more capacity.
- If a collection or your site is at a limit, the upload or create action is blocked. A message tells you who to contact.

## Export site data

On the **Site users** tab, click **Export site data** to download your site's documents, protocols, and chat history as a JSON file.
