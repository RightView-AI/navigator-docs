**Navigator** helps you work with protocol documents, lab manuals, investigator guides, and other trial materials through a chat interface with traceable sources.

Use this guide to learn how to:

- Sign in and navigate the app
- Ask questions in chat and review citations
- Upload and manage study document collections
- Save responses as artifacts and share them with your site
- Send answers to Principal Investigators via the inbox

## Open Navigator

Go to **[sites.rightview.ai](https://sites.rightview.ai)** to sign in.

## Who this guide is for

This documentation is written for site staff, medical monitors, and other users who interact with Navigator day to day. Admin-only features (user management, site analytics, sponsor dashboards) are summarized in [Roles & Permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions).

## Need help?

Email **research@rightview.ai** if you cannot sign in or need access to a study collection.
