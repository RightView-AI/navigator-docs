**Navigator** helps clinical research teams work with protocol documents, lab manuals, investigator guides, and other trial materials through a chat interface with traceable sources.

<DocQuickLinks />

## Open Navigator

Go to **[sites.rightview.ai](https://sites.rightview.ai)** to sign in.

## Who this guide is for

This guide is for site staff, medical monitors, and administrators using Navigator day to day. Site administrators will find collection management and [access control](/collections/restrict-access) documented here. Global and sponsor administrator features are summarized in [Roles & Permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions).

## Need help?

Email **research@rightview.ai** if you cannot sign in or need access to a study collection.
