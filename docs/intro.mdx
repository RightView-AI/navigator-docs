## Open Navigator

Go to **[sites.rightview.ai](https://sites.rightview.ai)** to sign in.

To work alongside your EDC or source documents, install the **[Rightview Navigator Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/abailmienbplhpgipgbdgendbdfpaeak)** and open it from the browser toolbar. See [Chrome side-panel extension](/extension/chrome-extension). If you sign in to Navigator in Chrome without the extension, Navigator shows a one-time **Get the Navigator extension** notice with an **Install** link.

## Who this guide is for

This guide is for site staff, medical monitors, and administrators using Navigator day to day. Site administrators will find collection management and [access control](/collections/manage-collections#restrict-collection-and-document-access) documented here. Global and sponsor administrator features are summarized in [Roles & Permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions).

## Need help?

Email **support@rightview.ai** if you cannot sign in or need access to a study collection. To check whether Navigator is running normally, see the [System Status](https://sites.rightview.ai/status) page.
