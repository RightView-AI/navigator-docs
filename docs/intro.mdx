**Navigator** helps clinical research teams work with protocol documents, lab manuals, investigator guides, and other trial materials through a chat interface with traceable sources.

Use this guide to learn how to:

- Sign in and navigate the app
- Ask questions in chat and review citations
- Upload and manage study document collections
- Save responses as artifacts and share them privately, with selected colleagues, or with your whole site
- Restrict who can use a collection or specific documents (site administrators)
- Send answers to Principal Investigators via the inbox

## Open Navigator

Go to **[sites.rightview.ai](https://sites.rightview.ai)** to sign in.

## Who this guide is for

This guide is for site staff, medical monitors, and other day-to-day Navigator users. Site administrators will also find steps for access control, collection rename, and exports under **Site settings**. Admin features such as user management, insights, and sponsor previews are summarized in [Roles & Permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions).

## Need help?

Email **research@rightview.ai** if you cannot sign in or need access to a study collection.
