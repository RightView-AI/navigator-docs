# Save and share artifacts

**Artifacts** are saved documents that you make from chat answers. Use them to keep verified answers, visit notes, or reference material that your team can use again.

## Save a chat answer as an artifact

1. In chat, find the answer.
2. Below the answer, click **Save**.
3. In the dialog, type a title.
4. Optional: click **Edit** to change the content. Click **Preview** to see the result. To add or change a table, see [Work with tables](#work-with-tables).
5. Click **Save**.

The button below the answer changes to **Saved**. The artifact is linked to the active study.

You can also ask chat to save an answer, for example *"Save this as a document titled Visit 2 checklist"*. A **Created artifact** link shows under the answer. Click it to open the artifact in **Study Documents**. To save two or more artifacts, name each one in the same message. Chat saves each one with its own title and content.

To save a spreadsheet, ask chat for one, for example *"Make a spreadsheet of the visit windows"*. Chat shows the table in the answer and saves it as a spreadsheet artifact. A spreadsheet artifact opens in a grid. See [Edit a spreadsheet artifact](#edit-a-spreadsheet-artifact).

## Find your artifacts

1. In the sidebar, click **Study Documents**.
2. Make sure the correct study shows at the top of the page.
3. Click the **Artifacts** filter chip.

Each artifact shows as a card with the **Artifact** label. The card shows who made it and if it is shared. Type in the **Search this study…** box to find an artifact by title.

You see your own artifacts and the artifacts that others shared with you.

## Open an artifact

Click the artifact card. The artifact opens in a window. The window shows who made the artifact and if it is shared.

The bottom of the window has **Download PDF** and **Download Word**. If the artifact has a table, it also has **Download Excel**. The creator also sees **Edit** and **Share**.

## Download or print an artifact

1. Click the **...** menu on the artifact card. You can also open the artifact.
2. Click **Download PDF**, **Download Word**, **Download Excel**, or **Print**. **Print** is only in the card menu.

Everyone who can see the artifact can download or print it.

- **Download Excel** shows only when the artifact has a table. Each table goes on its own sheet. A sheet takes its name from the heading above the table. A table without a heading goes on a sheet named **Table 1**, **Table 2**, and so on. The Excel file holds only the tables.
- Charts, stat tiles, timelines, and comparisons from a chat answer also show in the PDF, the Word file, and the printout. A chart shows as a picture. The Word file also puts the chart data in a table under the picture. Stat tiles, timelines, and comparisons show as tables.

## Share an artifact

Only the creator of an artifact and platform administrators can change its sharing.

1. Click the **...** menu on the artifact card, then click **Share**. You can also open the artifact and click **Share**.
2. In the **Share artifact** dialog, choose who can see it:
   - **Private** - only you. This is the default for a new artifact.
   - **Specific people** - select users at your site.
   - **Everyone at this site** - all users at your site.
3. Click **Save**.

You can open **Share** again at any time to change who can see the artifact.

## Edit an artifact

Only the creator of an artifact and platform administrators can edit it.

1. Click the **...** menu on the card, then click **Edit**. You can also open the artifact and click **Edit**. The **Edit artifact** dialog opens.
2. Change the title or the content.
3. Click **Save**.

### Work with tables

The editor toolbar has table controls.

1. To add a table, put the cursor where you want it and click **Insert table**. Navigator adds a table with three rows and three columns. The first row is the header row.
2. Click in a table cell. The **Row** and **Column** controls show in the toolbar.
3. Use **Add row above**, **Add row below**, or **Delete row** to change rows.
4. Use **Add column left**, **Add column right**, or **Delete column** to change columns.

The header row stays first. You cannot add a row above it or delete it. Point at a control to see its name.

### Edit a spreadsheet artifact

A spreadsheet artifact opens in a grid, not in the text editor. The first row holds the column headers.

1. Open the **Edit artifact** dialog.
2. Click a cell and type. Press **Tab**, **Enter**, or the arrow keys to move between cells.
3. To paste a range from Excel or Google Sheets, click the first cell and paste.
4. Use the **Row** and **Column** controls above the grid to add or delete rows and columns.
5. Click **Save**.

When you open a spreadsheet artifact without editing it, the grid is read-only.

## Delete an artifact

Only the creator of an artifact and platform administrators can delete it.

1. Click the **...** menu on the card.
2. Click **Delete**.

Navigator deletes the artifact right away. There is no confirmation step.
