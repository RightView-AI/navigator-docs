**Study Documents shortcuts, clearer Source QC findings, and faster, more reliable chat**

{/* truncate */}

### Study Documents

- **Schedule of Assessments card** - the saved schedule shows as a card above the study documents. The card shows **Draft** or **Approved**, the version, and the save date. Click it to open **Visits → Schedule of Assessments**.
- **Patient source documents** - source documents from Source QC show in their own section below the study documents. Each card shows the subject and visit, or the file name, and the review status. Click a card to open the document in Source QC.
- **Finance section** - the **Billing rules** card and the imported invoices show in a **Finance** section. Click a card to open the **Billing rules** tab or the **Invoiceables** tab.

### Source QC

- **More consistent findings** - Source QC reads the whole document before it checks each page. It also checks the document again at the end. It finds problems that need more than one page, such as dates that conflict or a subject number that changes.
- **Longer documents** - Source QC reviews up to 50 pages of a PDF. Before, the limit was 20 pages. If a PDF is longer, a notice shows how many pages Navigator reviewed.
- **Evidence for each finding** - a finding shows the text from the page that supports it, with its location.
- **Needs review list** - findings that Navigator is not sure about show in a separate **Needs review** list. They do not fail the document.
- **One finding for one problem** - when several checks report the same problem on a page, Navigator shows one finding.
- **Fewer false findings** - a page check no longer flags a calculated value that uses readings from other pages.
- **Clearer document list** - each document is one row. The title shows above the file name. Older uploads of the same file show under **N versions**.
- **Subject in the title** - when a document has no subject link, the title uses the subject identifier that Navigator reads from the page, for example **Subject 1001**. A subject link that you confirm replaces it.
- **Search** - use **Search names and comments…** to find a document by its name or by the text of a comment on a finding.
- **No slowdown from large documents** - Source QC analysis now runs outside the main app. A large document no longer slows chat. The results look the same.

### Chat

- **Faster multi-step answers** - when one answer needs several searches, Navigator runs them at the same time. It also skips a token-count step before each model step.
- **Fewer stalled answers** - when the model is slow to start an answer, Navigator sends a second request and uses the first reply. A slow model call no longer uses the whole five minutes of a run.
- **Citations show as chips** - three citation problems are fixed. A document name no longer shows as raw text. Math that starts with a digit shows as math. A citation that mixes a study document and a regulation shows as separate chips.
- **Regulatory search** - the search of regulatory documents uses the same Voyage-4 search as your study documents.
- **Help about modules** - when you ask chat why **Visits** or **Finance** does not show, the answer says that you need the module on your site and in your own list.

### Ask a Contact

- **Email replies are labeled as email** - when a contact answers by email, chat shows **PI response (email)**. The full text on hover says the sender is checked and not dual authenticated. **PI Verified** stays for answers that a person signs in the app.
- **Same label in Study Documents** - the card of an email answer says **Email response from** and the name of the contact.

### Uploads

- **Faster first upload** - Navigator starts the upload service when you point at an upload control. The first upload after a quiet period waits less.
- **One failed status check no longer ends an upload** - Navigator checks the upload again after a short wait when a status check fails in a temporary way.

### Administration

- **Scheduled data retention** - for a site that has a retention period, Navigator removes expired data every day. A site with no retention period keeps its data.

### Access limits

- **CTMS not reachable** - for a user with limited study access, chat does not show the EHR record of a patient when Navigator cannot check the patient's study in the CTMS. Chat says that it could not reach the CTMS.

### Fixes

- **Display name** - the **Display name** field in **Account settings** is no longer empty when you open it from the sidebar.
- **Rename in Manage collection** - a refresh of the study list no longer clears a Protocol ID or Sponsor name that you are typing.
