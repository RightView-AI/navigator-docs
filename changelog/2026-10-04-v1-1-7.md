**Study links to the CTMS, a richer Patients page, and faster, safer chat**

{/* truncate */}

### CTMS study link

- **Link a study to its CTMS study** - a site administrator picks the CTMS study for a collection in **Manage**, on the **Collection Settings** tab. Patients, Finance, and chat then read that study, even when the names differ.
- **Suggested match** - when a site administrator creates a collection on a site with a CTMS, Navigator suggests the matching CTMS study. Nothing links until you confirm.
- **Clear warnings** - Patients and Finance show a notice when a study has no link. Navigator also warns you before a new link stops another collection from reading the same CTMS study by name.
- **Safe relinking** - when you change a link, Navigator marks the billing rows from the old study for review in Finance.

### Patients page

- **Visit progress** - the patient page lists the EDC progress of each visit, for example **Entered**, **QC done**, or **Monitor reviewed**. A visit that is done in the CTMS but not in the EDC shows **Visit done, EDC pending**. The roster shows the same status next to the next visit.
- **Queries and appointments** - the patient page lists **Subject queries**, **Appointments**, and linked **Source QC** documents. The roster flags a subject with an **Open query**. A section shows only when your CTMS provides the data.
- **Complete roster** - the roster no longer skips subjects past the first 40. It shows how many subjects are **not checked yet** and gives a **Load more** button for them.
- **Correct next visit** - the next visit and the checklist follow the CTMS visit schedule when it differs from the Schedule of Assessments.

### Source QC and the extension

- **Link a document to a subject** - a Source QC document can link to a subject and a visit. Navigator suggests the link from the page header, and you click **Confirm**. Use **Change** or **Clear** to edit it later.
- **Easier tab choice** - in **Cross-check with EDC**, search tabs by title or site. Click **Use current tab** or **Refresh tabs**. The **EDC tab (optional)** list leaves out PDF and image tabs.
- **Welcome page on install** - the extension opens the Welcome page in a new tab after the first install. The side panel itself opens straight to Navigator.
- **Ask chat about the extension** - ask chat what the extension does or how to open it, and Navigator answers.

### Finance desk

- **Invoice headers** - the **Invoiceables** tab groups lines under their invoice header, with the invoice number, status, and due date.
- **Payables and subject payments** - the **Invoiceables** tab shows a **Payables** section. The patient detail view shows **Subject payments**. Each shows when your CTMS provides the data.

### Chat

- **Live answers** - the answer text appears while Navigator writes it, so you can start reading sooner.
- **Questions on CTMS data** - where your CTMS supports it, ask chat about subject queries, study documents, invoices, and payment totals. Lookups stay in the study you selected.
- **Confirm before a schedule is replaced** - chat never replaces an approved Schedule of Assessments by itself. It asks you to confirm first. A build that runs while an approval happens does not overwrite the approval.
- **Cleaner answers** - numbered lists, citations, and tables in answers show in the correct order and format.
- **Tables copy with right-aligned money** - when you copy a chat table into a spreadsheet or document, money columns stay right-aligned.
- **Ask a Contact fixes** - the contact list matches the study of the answer. If a send fails, the panel shows **Could not send. Try again.**

### Uploads

- **Choose who can see a file first** - in the **Upload** panel, **Who can see this?** now shows right after you add files, before **Next: Name Files →**. **Confidential** is its own choice with a **Members** field. The next step shows a **Visible to:** line.
- **Upload progress for personal documents** - when you add a personal document from chat, a status line above the message box shows progress, success, or the error.

### Sign-in and administration

- **Reset MFA for site staff** - on the **Site users** tab, a site administrator clicks the key icon next to a site editor, then clicks **Reset MFA**. The person sets up the authenticator app again at the next sign-in.
- **New link in place** - if a sign-in link has expired or is used, the page offers **Send a new link**.

### Access limits

- **Medical monitors stay in scope** - medical monitors see only their assigned studies in chat, Source QC, study documents, and amendments. They cannot change clinical or finance data.
- **Sealed studies stay sealed** - a user without access to a study no longer reaches it through artifacts, Source QC mentions, or chat lookups.
