**Charts in chat, clearer pre-screening and call scripts, safer sign-in and document access, and bug fixes**

{/* truncate */}

This release includes everything in the [v1.1.9 beta](/changelog/v1-1-9-beta). The items below are new since the beta.

### Chat

- **Pre-screening in chat** - describe a patient in chat and Navigator runs the same pre-screening check as the side panel. The result shows the verdict first, then the criteria in groups. See [Pre-screening in chat](/chat/asking-questions#pre-screening-in-chat).
- **Pre-screening with the captured screen** - when the side panel sends a captured page, the check uses the text of that page and keeps only evidence that quotes it.
- **Cut-off notice** - when a PDF patient record is too long, pre-screening tells you that Navigator cut it off.
- **Download from created artifacts** - the **Created artifact** line in chat has download options that fit the artifact, such as **Download Excel** for a table.

### Study documents

- **Previews for more file types** - Word (.docx), Markdown, CSV, and image files open in the preview on the **Study Documents** page. See [Card actions](/collections/manage-collections#card-actions).
- **Confirm before you delete** - deleting a document, a contact answer, or an artifact asks you to confirm.
- **Delete removes earlier versions** - when you delete the current version of a document, Navigator also deletes its earlier versions. The dialog says so.
- **Personal uploads over 32 MB** - personal documents upload straight to storage, like study documents. A file over the limit is refused before the upload starts.
- **Site-wide documents** - a site administrator can delete a site-wide document and choose the studies it applies to from its card.
- **AI curator switch** - a site administrator can turn the AI curator on or off for a study in **Collection Settings**. See [Turn the AI curator on or off](/collections/manage-collections#turn-the-ai-curator-on-or-off-site-administrators).

### Security and access

- **Limits on wrong attempts** - Navigator counts wrong passwords and authenticator codes for each account and waits after 5 wrong attempts in 15 minutes. See [Limits on wrong attempts](/getting-started/sign-in#limits-on-wrong-attempts).
- **Password confirmation** - a wrong password when you sign a contact answer or change your password keeps you signed in. The page shows how long to wait after too many tries.
- **Expired passwords** - a user with MFA enters the authenticator code before Navigator accepts the new password.
- **Access lists stay in place** - a new version of a document, or a rename of a study, keeps the access list. A user who cannot see a document cannot delete or rename it.
- **Source QC runs as you** - Source QC and Visits search only the documents that you can see. Navigator records a Source QC read in the audit log.
- **Chat checks access again** - chat search results are checked against document access before they reach an answer.
- **Audit trail** - audit entries record where in the app an action happened. The **Audit Log** can filter by that surface and shows a detail view.
- **Sponsor admins** - the chat Insights tool is never available to a sponsor administrator.

### Fixes

- **Citation boxes** - a citation box on a page that has its origin at the bottom left is now in the right place.
- **Late uploads** - a late upload no longer brings back a study that you deleted.
- **Upload staging** - a direct upload cannot claim the same staged file twice. A new document no longer fails when a check refuses it.
- **Schedule of Assessments** - a rebuild keeps the approved flag that the new version inherits.
- **Page-limit questions** - a question about which pages the extension reads goes to help.
